# Separation operations

Select the operation and objective before proposing plots. Confirm phase identities, composition basis, flowrates, pressures, temperature, equilibrium data and any solvent-free/solute-free coordinates.

## Candidate outputs

- Distillation: compositions, recovery, relevant material balances; equilibrium and operating lines and ideal stage stepping only when the selected model's assumptions and data are met. Reflux, feed condition, total/partial condenser, and reboiler counting conventions affect stage counts.
- Absorption: gas/liquid balance, removal, appropriate equilibrium/operating relations. Dilute mole fractions and solute-free ratios are different coordinates; keep model and plotted axes consistent.
- Extraction: distribution relation, phase balances, recovery and stages with a justified phase-equilibrium model. Triangular diagrams, tie lines and changing phase flows cannot be replaced universally by a straight operating line.
- Membrane separation: flux `J=V/(A t)` for a specified collected permeate volume/time interval, rejection `1−Cp/Cf` with a stated concentration basis, and recovery with an explicit definition. Pressure-normalized permeability and concentration polarization require additional conditions/models.
- Constant-pressure cake filtration: inspect cumulative filtrate volume and elapsed time. Use a time-per-volume versus volume fit only over records with positive volume and when the assigned cake model and pressure conditions apply. Deriving cake and medium resistances requires the model coefficients, pressure difference, area, viscosity, and solids basis. Preserve the time and volume zero references.
- Drying: plot moisture content versus time on a stated wet or dry basis, with mass tare and dry-solids basis documented. Derive a drying-rate curve only if required. Specify the differencing or smoothing method and area basis, and check whether sampling resolution supports identifying distinct drying regimes.

“Separation efficiency” is not a universal equation. Identify whether the objective means recovery, purity, rejection, stage efficiency, or another quantity. Stage stepping and nonlinear equilibrium interpolation need traceable formulas/steps and agreed interpolation; do not return a stage count unsupported by the diagram or method.

## Discussion prompts

Show actual purity/recovery tradeoffs, balance gaps or flux changes. Ask about sampled phases, flow stability, equilibrium time, entrainment, pressure records and membrane condition only when relevant. Distinguish an observed trend from its possible cause; e.g. declining flux alone does not identify fouling as the exclusive mechanism.
