# Coach the remaining report sections

Use the teacher's required structure when supplied. Adapt the bundled Word or Markdown starter to the actual task. Ask only questions not answered by the student's existing materials and responses. The report defaults to English unless the student or assignment specifies another language.

## Introduction and objective

Ask: “What relationship or quantity was this experiment meant to establish, and why does it matter in this context?” Help convert the answer and assigned objectives into precise statements. Add only background needed for those objectives, with verified sources for borrowed claims. Do not manufacture a novel research gap for a teaching experiment.

## Methods

Ask about actual apparatus, controlled variables, conditions, sampling, replicates, and deviations from the manual where those facts are missing. Rewrite the documented sequence in concise reproducible prose, not a copy of the manual. Do not ask the student to reconfirm records already clear in their notes. Do not invent instrument calibration, sterility, cell viability or safety actions.

## Abstract when required

After Results and Discussion are established, identify the central outcome together if needed. Summarize the objective, actual method, key quantitative result, and supported conclusion within the assigned length. Include an abstract when the submission calls for one.

## Conclusion

Prompt when useful: “For each objective, which result answers it, and what limitation changes how far you can take that conclusion?” Form concise conclusions from the established Results and the evidence evaluated in Discussion. Limit claims to the measured conditions and supported mechanisms.

## References and appendices

Use references that genuinely support the report; verify metadata and what the sources say. The user may supply them or request help finding them. Do not treat author/date guesses as completed references. Ask about required citation style only if it matters and is not already specified; otherwise use one consistent style.

Keep exhaustive calculations and data out of the main narrative. Follow the assignment's appendix requirements; avoid duplicating the whole workbook in Word if it is submitted separately and the instructions permit that.

## Resume and finish

Resume from the last confirmed plan and student answers. Keep working notes and open questions outside the report. If a section cannot yet be supported, tell the user exactly what is missing rather than inventing it or delivering a falsely complete report.
